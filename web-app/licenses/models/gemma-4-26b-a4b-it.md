## Why this is not in the generated notice

`src/generated/third-party.json` and `NOTICE.txt` are written by
`npm run licenses:notice`, which walks the installed production tree. They
attribute what this application **redistributes** — npm packages, and the font
files served from this origin.

Gemma is neither. No weights are downloaded, vendored or served; the app calls a
hosted endpoint. The Gemma Terms therefore bind **use**, not redistribution, and
there is nothing in the shipped bundle to attribute. This file records the
obligation the generator cannot see.

## What the terms require of this use

- The output may be used freely, including commercially; Google claims no rights
  in it.
- Use remains subject to the Prohibited Use Policy above.
- Because no weights are distributed, the terms' distribution obligations —
  passing on the use restrictions, and supplying a copy of the terms to
  downstream recipients — are not triggered.
